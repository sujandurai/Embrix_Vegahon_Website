WHAT IS
EMBRIX '26 – VEGATHON?

EMBRIX '26 – VEGATHON is a 48-Hour National-Level Hardware Hackathon conducted by Bannari Amman Institute of Technology, organized by the Industry Exploration and Collaboration Cell (IECC), in collaboration with the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), Thiruvananthapuram.

More than just a competition, EMBRIX '26 – VEGATHON is a launchpad for innovators to transform bold ideas into working prototypes. Focused on hardware innovation, embedded systems, Edge AI, and indigenous technology, participants will solve real-world challenges using the VEGA Development Board with mentorship from industry and academic experts.

TRACK 01

TRACK 1 – HARDWARE INNOVATION

Design, prototype, and validate innovative hardware solutions using the VEGA Processor platform to solve real-world challenges in the following domains:

Safety, Disaster & Emergency Response
Healthcare & Assistive Technology
Smart Agriculture & Food Security
Smart Cities & Infrastructure
Fiction in Real Life – Enhanced Gadgets
TRACK 02

TRACK 2 – EDGE AI & TINYML CHALLENGE

Develop intelligent, low-power AI solutions powered by Edge AI and TinyML using the VEGA Processor platform. Participants are encouraged to build innovative on-device AI applications in the following domains:

Predictive Analytics & Anomaly Detection
AI for Smart IoT & Connected Devices
Intelligent Monitoring & Automation
Edge AI for Real-Time Decision Making
Autonomous Systems & Intelligent Robotics
RECOMMENDED VEGA PLATFORMS

VEGA ARIES IoT DEVELOPMENT BOARD
